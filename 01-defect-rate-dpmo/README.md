## Business problem
A process had a high share of wrongly handled items, but nobody had measured it, so everyone assumed it was not worth fixing.

## What this project shows
- Joining two process stages and removing duplicates
- Matching results against an escalations dataset
- DPMO (defects per million opportunities) per employee
- Flags above a threshold and stricter steps for repeat cases
- Reports by manager, shift, tenure and employment type
